# Third-party data and notices — ai-service

## NB Uttale (Språkbanken, National Library of Norway)

- **What:** pronunciation lexicon of Norwegian Bokmål — word forms with NoFAbet / IPA transcriptions,
  stress and toneme, five dialect areas, written and spoken norm.
- **Used for:** naming the sounds and the toneme of a word in the voice review (plan 88). Loaded into
  `pronunciation_lexicon` by `prisma/lexicon/import-nb-uttale.ts`; today the østnorsk files
  `e_written_pronunciation_lexicon.csv` and `e_spoken_pronunciation_lexicon.csv` (release of March 2023).
- **Source:** https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/en/resource-catalogue/oai-nb-no-sbr-79/
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) —
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No attribution is required; it is given here as a
  courtesy and to record where the data comes from.
- The data files are not stored in this repository.
